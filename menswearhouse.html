Men’s Wearhouse · Retail innovation

Twelve ideas.
Sixty customers.
Two survivors.

A Store of the Future programme where customers — not the team — decided which concepts were worth building.

Role
Design Director
Client
Men’s Wearhouse
Year
2019
Platform
In-store · Interactive retail
Responsibilities
Research, concepting, UX, visual design, prototyping

01 · The method

Let customers do the narrowing.

We developed twelve digital concepts, then took them to a UX research think tank in Chicago rather than defending them in a boardroom.

02 · What we watched for

Not what people said. What they did.

Board 02 Concept testing criteria

We asked Which ideas made immediate sense?
We asked Which solved an actual problem?
We asked Which created confusion?
We asked Which genuinely improved the shopping trip?
Killed Concepts that needed explaining before use.
Survived Shirt Wall — guided discovery on a wall of product.
Survived Lay Down Table — building the outfit in the open.

03 · Shirt Wall

Two paths to the same goal.

A five-question style quiz for customers who wanted guidance, and a filter wheel across ten categories for those who already knew.
