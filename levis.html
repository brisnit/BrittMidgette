01 · Research

Not a survey. Not a persona built from assumptions.

One working tailor, on the floor, walking me through the job — the daily rituals of the team and the moments where they meet the customer.

02 · Method

Research. Sketch. Test. Ship.

Rough wireframes explored the concept fast, then a movement study validated the interactions in physical space before anything was built.

03 · The movement study

A tool that works the way tailors actually move.

Board 03 Physical validation

Why A tablet tool on a shop floor is used standing, mid-task, with hands full

Tested How far the tailor travels between steps
Tested What can be done one-handed
Result Interactions matched the real choreography of the job.
Result Fewer steps between the customer and the record.
