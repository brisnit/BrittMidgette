SDG&E · Operational intelligence

Seeing risk
before it
becomes reality.

For a utility in wildfire country, wind and humidity aren’t a forecast — they decide where crews go and what gets watched. We turned thousands of data points into a tool readable at a glance.

Role
Design Director
Client
SDG&E
Year
2019
Region
Southern California
Responsibilities
Research, product strategy, UX, visual design

The challenge

The problem was never displaying weather data.

It was turning thousands of readings into something a field crew, a meteorologist and an operations lead could each act on — outdoors, quickly, under changing conditions.
