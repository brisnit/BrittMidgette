Overview

CompTIA is a large-scale learning platform transformation, rescued mid-flight and rebuilt around an editorial direction that broke from every convention in IT certification. The CEO had a clear vision that challenged the direction of the previous design team. We were brought in to decipher that vision, translate it into a cohesive experience, and bring it to life.

The challenge

A stalled transformation, and a vision that only existed on a whiteboard.

We joined as a development partner. As the design problems surfaced, I embedded with CompTIA’s team, then we took over the design work entirely.

Interpreting the vision

Turning sketches into a direction the whole team could build against.

The CEO had a clear instinct for what the experience should feel like. Our job was to make it concrete, defensible and buildable.

What I led

  • Executive translation. Worked directly from the CEO’s sketches and intent, and turned them into a direction the whole team could argue with and build against.
  • Design direction. Set the editorial visual language and held it across the homepage, Explore Careers, certifications, AutoOps+, enterprise and academic.
  • Information architecture. Restructured the certification and careers content so a student could find a path rather than a catalogue.
  • Design system. Built the component set and page templates that let the work scale past the screens we designed.
  • Delivery alignment. Embedded with CompTIA’s team and worked to development-ready, approved files rather than handing over mockups.

The outcome

We quickly translated CEO Todd Thibodeaux’s vision into a live digital experience that helps thousands of students access CompTIA’s services and earn IT certifications.
