Bose · Digital retail

Every pixel had
a physical
consequence.

Digital experiences designed inside an existing physical display system — where screen size, viewing angle and the product itself set the terms.

Role
Design Director
Client
Bose
Year
2024
Platform
In-store · QR · Bose.com · Retail partners
Responsibilities
Immersion, research synthesis, UX, visual design

01 · The constraint

We couldn’t ask the room to accommodate the interface.

The displays, the hardware and the retail system already existed. That constraint became part of the design process rather than an obstacle to it.

Board 01 What the physical system dictated

Fixed Screen dimensions and orientation
Fixed Product placement on the fixture
Fixed Viewing angle and reach
Fixed Interaction areas and hardware
Fixed The retail environment around it
So Design on the rails of the display, not around them.
So Let the product stay the hero.
So Useful without becoming distracting.

02 · Research

Years of insight already existed. Our job was to find what mattered.

Rather than starting research from zero, we immersed ourselves in what Bose already knew about shopping behaviour, demonstrations and the questions customers ask before buying.
