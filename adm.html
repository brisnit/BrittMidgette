ADM · Global digital transformation

One company,
two hundred
countries.

A global food and nutrition business whose digital estate had grown faster than any structure could hold. We audited it, restructured it, and built the system to keep it coherent.

Role
Design Director
Client
ADM
Year
2022
Platform
Web · 200+ countries
Responsibilities
Strategy, content audit, IA, design system, UI direction

The challenge

Scale had become the problem.

Years of independent growth left overlapping content, competing navigation logic, and no shared language between markets.

Before — what we found

Thousands of pages accumulated across markets
The same product described differently in each region
Navigation logic that changed between sections
No shared component vocabulary

After — what we built

One architecture, market-aware but consistent
A single content model per product type
Predictable navigation everywhere
One design system across the estate
