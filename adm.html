Overview

A global food and nutrition business whose digital estate had grown faster than any structure could hold. We audited it, restructured it, and built the system to keep it coherent.

The challenge

Scale had become the problem.

Years of independent growth left overlapping content, competing navigation logic, and no shared language between markets.

Structure

A content model that survives translation.

Templates designed so a market team can publish without inventing new patterns.

The system

One language, many markets.

Tokens and components that hold the brand together while letting each region speak to its own audience.
