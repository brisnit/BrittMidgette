Under Armour · Future retail

What could
a store do for
an athlete?

Not a better shop. A Human Performance Center — where performance data, elite training expertise and physical space combine to make someone measurably better.

Role
Experience Director
Client
Under Armour
Year
2018
Platform
Physical retail · Connected fitness
Responsibilities
Experience strategy, concept development, journey

The reframe

The question changed, and everything followed.

Instead of asking

How do we sell more product in the store?
How do we get more people through the door?
How do we make retail feel modern?

We asked

How do we create more value for the athlete?
What can this brand do that no retailer can?
If we help someone improve, does commerce follow?

01 · Strategy

Three ideas the whole concept hangs from.

Under Armour already had athletes, trainers, performance data and connected fitness. The opportunity was to combine them.

Board 01 Human Performance Center

Innovation Use technology and data to make the invisible parts of performance visible.
Sport Let everyday athletes feel the intensity and mindset of elite competition.
