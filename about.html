About Britt

Background

I work at the seam between design and engineering, where good ideas either become real products or quietly fall apart.

Most of my career has been spent on the strategy and experience side of agency work, leading design teams for global brands including Bose, Under Armour, MAC Cosmetics, LEGOLAND, CompTIA, ADM, SAG-AFTRA. The work has ranged from enterprise platforms carrying real regulatory weight to connected retail environments and a park-wide game played by children who never saw a screen.

My background spans digital strategy, user experience and visual design, with deep and repeated experience building and scaling design systems that keep large organisations coherent. I don’t hand off at the mockup. I write the front-end code that turns a system into something people actually use.

That end-to-end fluency means fewer translation losses between teams, faster paths from concept to production, and design decisions that hold up once they meet the realities of the browser.

More recently I have been building products of my own: a learning intelligence platform, a CRM, and a commerce tool for independent sellers, designing and shipping each one end to end.

Alongside the work itself I have co-led a design practice, handling staffing, scoping, budgets and the standards that protect quality between a design file and a shipped build. I think in systems, communicate in plain language, and ship. You can read more about how I approach design, or what I can take on across a project.
