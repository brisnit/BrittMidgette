Samsung · Immersive retail

A showroom
you could
scan into.

Premium appliances brought to life on the store floor through an AR experience that needed no app, no install and no explanation.

Role
Design Director
Client
Samsung
Year
2023
Platform
In-store · Mobile web AR
Responsibilities
AR strategy, experience design, build

The challenge

Every install is a door people don’t walk through.

Native AR meant an app-store detour in the middle of a shopping trip. The experience had to start at the speed of curiosity.

02 · Designing for the floor

A retail environment is not a usability lab.

Board 02 Constraints → decisions

Constraint People are standing, moving, and not alone.
Constraint Lighting and space vary store to store.
Constraint No one reads instructions on a shop floor.
Decision One gesture to start. No onboarding.
Decision Product does the explaining, not the UI.
Decision Every state readable at arm’s length.

Impact

0 Installs required
QR The entire entry point
In-store Built for the floor, tested on it
