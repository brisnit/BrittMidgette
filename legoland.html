LEGOLAND · Themed entertainment

A hidden world,
layered over
a real park.

A park-wide game that made the physical environment respond to a child — without putting another screen in front of them.

Role
Experience Director
Client
LEGOLAND · Merlin Entertainments
Park
LEGOLAND California
Platform
In-park · RFID · Mobile app
Responsibilities
Experience strategy, hidden journey, concept development

The governing constraint

No kid-facing screen time.

The digital layer had to make the physical park more interesting, not compete with it. The RFID keepsake became the interface.

01 · The core loop

Tap. Unlock. Play. Keep going.

One physical object, recognised everywhere, turning separate attractions into a single continuous adventure.

Scroll the flow →

02 · Two audiences, one system

Kids play. Parents orchestrate.

Board 02 Audience split

For kids The park is the interface. No app, no menus, no account to manage.
For kids Discover · unlock · build · race · collect
For parents Find participating locations and see what remains.
For parents Track progress and achievements across the day.
Design risk Any screen handed to a child breaks the premise.
Design risk Parents still need visibility without becoming the interface.

Impact

1 Physical key, recognised park-wide
2 Audiences served by one system
0 Screens required of a child

Kids didn’t need to understand RFID, identity or connected systems. They needed to understand that the park knew who they were.
